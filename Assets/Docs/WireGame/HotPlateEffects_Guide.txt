Hot Plate Effects (Candle + Ice Flask) - Setup Guide
=====================================================

PURPOSE
-------
When all 4 wires are connected with the hot plate snapped in,
items on the hot plate start melting. Two flask experiments:

  Candle Flask: candle sinks & tilts, melted wax liquid rises, flame goes out
  Ice Flask:    ice shrinks & fades, water rises

Both are children of HotPlate so they move together when dragged.
Activate/deactivate whichever flask you want in the Inspector.


SPRITES YOU NEED
----------------
Candle Flask (4 sprites):
  - flask_glass.png       — Flask/beaker container shape
  - candle.png            — Candle (will rotate + sink)
  - wax_liquid.png        — Melted wax pool (warm color, e.g. orange/cream)
  - flame.png             — Small flame on top of candle (optional)

Ice Flask (3 sprites):
  - flask_glass.png       — Same flask shape (reuse from candle)
  - ice.png               — Ice cube/block (translucent blue)
  - water.png             — Water (blue liquid)

Tip: Make liquid/water sprites tall enough to fill the flask.
They start hidden and rise upward during the melt animation.


HIERARCHY
---------
  HotPlate                   ← DraggableDevice + BoxCollider2D + SpriteRenderer
    ├── CandleFlask          ← CandleMeltEffect script
    │   ├── FlaskGlass       ← SpriteRenderer (flask_glass.png, Order: 3)
    │   ├── Candle           ← SpriteRenderer (candle.png, Order: 2)
    │   ├── Liquid           ← SpriteRenderer (wax_liquid.png, Order: 1)
    │   └── Flame            ← SpriteRenderer (flame.png, Order: 2)
    │
    └── IceFlask             ← IceMeltEffect script
        ├── FlaskGlass       ← SpriteRenderer (flask_glass.png, Order: 3)
        ├── Ice              ← SpriteRenderer (ice.png, Order: 2)
        └── Water            ← SpriteRenderer (water.png, Order: 1)

Note: Only one flask should be active at a time. Disable the other
in the Inspector (uncheck the GameObject checkbox). You can swap
which is active between play sessions, or add a selection UI later.

Sorting order: Glass on top (3) so it visually contains the items.
Candle/Ice in the middle (2). Liquid/Water in the back (1).


SETUP STEPS
-----------
Step 1: Create CandleFlask under HotPlate
  - Select HotPlate in the hierarchy
  - Create child empty GameObject → name it "CandleFlask"
  - Position it where you want the flask to sit on the hot plate
    (e.g., localPosition (0, 0.5, 0) to sit on top)

Step 2: Create CandleFlask children
  - Under CandleFlask, create 4 child GameObjects:
    FlaskGlass, Candle, Liquid, Flame
  - Add SpriteRenderer to each, assign sprites
  - Set sorting orders: FlaskGlass=3, Candle=2, Liquid=1, Flame=2
  - Position Candle inside the flask
  - Position Liquid at the BOTTOM of the flask (it will rise up)
  - Position Flame on top of the candle

Step 3: Add CandleMeltEffect to CandleFlask
  - Select CandleFlask → Add Component → CandleMeltEffect
  - Drag references:
    Candle field  → Candle child
    Liquid field  → Liquid child
    Flame field   → Flame child (optional, leave empty to skip)
  - Adjust settings:
    Melt Duration       — seconds for the full melt (default 3)
    Candle Sink Amount  — how far candle drops (default 0.5)
    Candle Rotation     — tilt in degrees (default -15)
    Liquid Rise Amount  — how far liquid rises (default 0.5)

Step 4: Create IceFlask under HotPlate
  - Same process as CandleFlask
  - Create child empty "IceFlask" under HotPlate
  - Create children: FlaskGlass, Ice, Water
  - Add sprites and sorting orders

Step 5: Add IceMeltEffect to IceFlask
  - Select IceFlask → Add Component → IceMeltEffect
  - Drag references:
    Ice field   → Ice child
    Water field → Water child
  - Adjust settings:
    Melt Duration      — seconds for full melt (default 3)
    Water Rise Amount  — how far water rises (default 0.5)

Step 6: Disable one flask
  - In the Inspector, uncheck the IceFlask GameObject (or CandleFlask)
  - Only the active flask will show and animate
  - Swap which is active to test different experiments

Step 7: Test
  - Play the scene
  - Drag HotPlate into SnapZone (flask children move with it)
  - Connect all 4 wires
  - Active flask should start its melt animation:
    Candle: wax appears, candle tilts and sinks, flame disappears
    Ice: water appears, ice shrinks and fades away


ANIMATION DETAILS
-----------------
Both effects use SmoothStep easing (slow start, accelerates, slow end).

Candle melt:
  - Liquid starts hidden, becomes visible, rises by liquidRiseAmount
  - Candle moves down by candleSinkAmount and rotates by candleRotation degrees
  - Flame disappears at the end of the animation
  - All happens over meltDuration seconds

Ice melt:
  - Water starts hidden, becomes visible, rises by waterRiseAmount
  - Ice scales from full size to zero and fades from opaque to transparent
  - Ice GameObject is deactivated at the end
  - All happens over meltDuration seconds


ADVANCED: SPRITE MASKING (optional polish)
------------------------------------------
For cleaner liquid containment (liquid doesn't overflow the flask):
  1. Add a SpriteMask component to FlaskGlass
  2. Set FlaskGlass sprite as the mask shape
  3. On the Liquid/Water SpriteRenderer, set "Mask Interaction" to
     "Visible Inside Mask"
  4. This clips the liquid to only show inside the flask shape

This is optional — the basic version works fine without masking.
